        Fine print

        Prices are US monthly list prices before tax for new subscribers, from each service's own pricing page. Your bill can differ: Disney+ and Hulu move existing members to the new price from their first bill on or after October 21, 2026. Edit any price to match yours. A higher plan includes the lower one (Ultimate includes Premium, Essential and PC Game Pass; PS Plus Premium includes Extra and Essential), so a month on both only counts the higher one.

        Statement import recognizes well-known streaming and gaming names, plus any charge under $80 that repeats about monthly. It reads only the date, description and amount, on your device.

        The time estimate subtracts sleep, work, travel, school, kids, and chores from a 168-hour week, then counts about 60% of what is left for shows and games. Defaults follow the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics American Time Use Survey 2025 (released June 2026): full-time workers have about 4 hours of leisure a day and spend about 2.4 of them on TV and games.

        Annual plans can beat rotating if you would use the service for more than about seven months of the year. Rotation works best on monthly plans you can cancel and rejoin.

        When you search a title, only the text you type is sent to TVMaze.
